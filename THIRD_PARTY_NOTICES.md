# Third-party materials and access

The project licenses cover Kang Wang's original material. They grant no new
rights in third-party names, citations, notices, legal texts or embedded fonts.
Individual figure PDFs contain font subsets whose rights remain with their
providers. No standalone font, model, tokenizer, dependency distribution or
dataset text is included.

GLUE resources have task-specific terms; consult the original SST-2 and QNLI
providers as well as the pinned GLUE card. BoolQ's provider specifies
CC BY-SA 3.0. The DeBERTa and RoBERTa provider cards specify MIT. These labels
describe upstream resources and do not extend the project's grant to them.
Pinned identities and access routes are in `data_or_data_access/README.md`.
Example identifiers refer to public benchmark rows and may be linked to those
rows; no claim of anonymization is made.

The MIT template includes the project copyright notice. The CC BY 4.0 legal
text is reproduced unmodified. Creative Commons provides its legal texts under
CC0; its trademarks retain their separate status.
