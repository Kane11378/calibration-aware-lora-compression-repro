# Finite-calibration dependency-support intervention

The already-completed intervention holds trained source models, retained
singular directions, deployment rank, the locked H1 estimator, compensation
solver and source-reference endpoint fixed. The calibration sizes are exactly
64, 128, 256 and 512. Reduced sizes use the first 64/128/256 positions of the
five prespecified PCG64 permutations with seeds 202610401--202610405.
The memberships are nested within each trajectory and matched by raw input
across the six BoolQ sources. N512 is one unique full-pool reference.
The archived N512 ridge is reused at every reduced size for the same source
and rank. No labels, task losses or held-out outcomes select complexity.

All eleven original sources remain in the estimator scaling table. The six
BoolQ sources retain C1/S1/H1/C2 coefficient conditions at ranks 8/4/2, with
calibration-independent M0 reused from its archived condition. The complete
stored evidence has 176 estimator rows, 1152 coefficient records, 1170 unique
raw-KL science rows and 1440 display rows including explicitly marked M0 reuse.
The 1080 reduced-size coefficient/endpoint conditions are not independent
replications. The raw endpoint is KL from the source distribution to the
candidate distribution on fixed validation inputs. Distances to each method's
own N512 coefficient vector are normalized by the retained source spectrum.

The descriptive reduced-size contrasts have H1 below M0 in 90/90 equal-rank
trajectory comparisons and below S1 in 20/90. Within/cross weights are
nonmonotonic in 26/55 and 24/55 sequences; H1 raw KL is nonmonotonic in 12/30.
Ranks and trajectories do not add independent source replications. All adverse
comparisons, seed607's majority-class micro-scale and seed809's behavior remain
visible. This intervention supports calibration-derived dependency shrinkage;
it does not impose monotonicity on every realized source/subset. The same
frozen memberships are reused by the G1 comparison described separately.
